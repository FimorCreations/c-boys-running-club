Safety Guide

Safety guide

We run as a pack. Stay with your pace group, carry water on longer sessions, and tell a captain if you peel off early. Session meeting details for each day are shown in Today’s Session.

  • Listen to session leads on route changes
  • Use sidewalks / track lanes where marked
  • Wear reflective kit for early mornings
  • In an emergency, call local services first, then message the WhatsApp group

FAQ

Common questions

Is membership really free?

Yes. R0 forever. No trial period, no hidden fees. You get the weekly plan, group sessions, and community access at no cost.

Do I need to be fast to join?

No. Every pace is welcome — from first 5K to marathon training. We group by effort so nobody gets left behind or held back.

Where and when do we meet?

Meeting details change with the plan — check Today’s Session for the current meet line, or ask in the WhatsApp group.

What should I bring?

Running shoes, water, and reflective kit for early mornings. On long-run days, carry extra fluid and a phone for the WhatsApp group.

How do I join the WhatsApp group?

Join directly here: C-Boys WhatsApp group. You can also email Cboysrunningclub@gmail.com.
