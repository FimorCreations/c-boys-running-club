Who we are

Built for runners.
Open to everyone.

C-Boys Running Club is a Kimberley pack that trains together, shares plans for free, and makes space for every pace — from first kilometres to race day.

Our mission

To make consistent running feel possible for anyone in Kimberley — by removing fees, sharing clear weekly plans, and showing up as a pack that leaves no runner behind.

Show up. Run with us. Free forever.

Our values

These are the standards we hold on every session — on the track, on the road, and in the group chat.

01

Open pack

Every pace is welcome. Beginners, comeback runners, and seasoned athletes train in the same club without hierarchy around speed.

02

Free access

Membership stays R0. Plans, sessions, and community support should not depend on what someone can pay.

03

Show up

Consistency beats perfection. We value the runners who keep returning — rain, shine, or slow weeks included.

04

Look after each other

We run as a pack. Pace groups stay together, captains communicate, and nobody gets left without a plan B.

05

Train with purpose

Easy days stay easy. Hard days have a point. The weekly plan is built so progress feels clear, not chaotic.

06

Keep it local

We’re rooted in Kimberley. The club exists to grow a running culture here — one session, one invite, one kilometre at a time.
